What to weigh before you remodel a Rochester primary bathroom.

Can a Rochester primary bathroom have both a walk-in shower and a freestanding tub?

Often, if the room allows comfortable space around both. We measure the bathroom and show you layouts in 3D, so you can see how the shower, tub and vanity fit before you decide. The Stony Pointe bathroom above has both.

Is a floating double vanity a good choice for a primary bath?

For two people sharing the room, usually. Each person gets their own sink and counter space, and because the vanity is off the floor, more of the floor shows and the room looks larger. In Stony Pointe we chose one in a warm wood finish.

When can we meet with a designer if we live in Rochester?

We come to your Rochester home for the free measure. The design meeting follows at our Chesterfield showroom, open Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm and Saturday 9am to 3pm, where you review your 3D renderings and choose tile, vanity and fixtures in person.
