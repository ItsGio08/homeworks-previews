KITCHEN

White Shaker kitchen with a black island

Middlecreek Estates, Macomb Township · near 22 Mile and Romeo Plank

Modern farmhouse meets transitional: ceiling-height white cabinets, a black island under three pendants and warm wood accents.

Read the project

This kitchen blends modern farmhouse and transitional style, with a bright white backdrop, bold black accents, and warm natural wood.

White Shaker cabinets extend to the ceiling with crown molding and brass-toned hardware. A large black island anchors the room, topped with a white countertop featuring subtle gray veining. Three oversized black pendant lights highlight the island, while woven counter stools add texture and casual seating.

White subway tile keeps the backsplash simple, and wood accents on the range hood, floating shelves, and built-in bench soften the black-and-white palette. Stainless steel appliances, under-cabinet lighting, and light wood-toned flooring complete the space.

The overall feel is bright, welcoming, and polished, with plenty of room for cooking and gathering.

Macomb Township
remodeling questions.

Answers from leaking showers, opened kitchens and 1990s homes we have remodeled here.

Where is HomeWorks based in Macomb?

Our warehouse and field operations are at 51445 Industrial Dr in Macomb, open weekdays 8am to 4pm, and our design showroom is at 49570 Gratiot Ave. in Chesterfield. Co-owner John Gelfusa has shared remodeling advice in Forbes, Martha Stewart, Bob Vila, Family Handyman and The Spruce. See our press features ↗

My Macomb Township shower is leaking or failed early. Can you rebuild it?

Yes. In Legacy Farms a shower failed after about five years, so we rebuilt just the shower with correct waterproofing behind the new tile. In Sumpter Forest a leak was reaching the basement, so we gutted the room and built a new waterproofed tile shower. We look at what is behind the tile first, then recommend the smaller or larger fix.

Can a small Macomb Township bathroom trade its tub for a walk-in shower?

Yes. In a 5-by-12-foot Westminster guest bathroom we replaced the old steel tub with a full walk-in shower and hung the vanity about 10 inches off the floor, which opened up the room. Subway tile carried across the walls ties the shower and the rest of the bath together.

Can you remove a wall to open up my kitchen?

Yes, when the home’s structure allows it. We check whether the wall is load-bearing and what plumbing, electrical or HVAC runs through it, then handle structural support, engineering and permits as the project requires. In Creekside, we opened a kitchen to the dining room with a support beam and rerouted HVAC.

What should I expect when remodeling a 1990s Macomb Township home?

Many homes built here in the 1990s have OSB subfloors that got wet during construction and now feel soft, green board behind shower tile instead of a waterproof backer, and weak waterproofing. We check the subfloor and joists before tiling and replace what is damaged. Read our 1990s home guide ↗
