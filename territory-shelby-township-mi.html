PLAN YOUR PROJECT

Shelby Township
remodeling questions.

Answers drawn from our Diegel Farms, Springhill and Silent Woods projects.

Can my Shelby Township kitchen fit an island with a cooktop?

Often, if there is enough clearance around it. In Diegel Farms we planned an island that holds an induction cooktop, storage, prep space and seating. We measure the room first, then show you in 3D where the cooking zone goes and how much walking space the island leaves.

Should I keep the tub in my Shelby Township guest bathroom?

It depends on who uses the room. If no one bathes there, a walk-in shower is usually easier to use and to clean. In Springhill we replaced an alcove tub with a tiled walk-in shower, frameless glass and a double vanity. Think through access and storage before you decide.

Can you replace the tile floors in a late-1990s Shelby Township home?

Yes. In a 1999 Silent Woods home we replaced 12-by-12-inch tile with 24-by-48-inch tile. Larger tile needs a flat, sound subfloor, planned transitions to other rooms and movement joints, so we check those before any tile is ordered.
