THE SMART WAY TO REMODEL

See the design first.
Then see the price.

Your detailed quote comes after the design meeting, once the layout and materials are chosen. So the price you read is for the room you actually picked.

See the full process ↗

  1. 1. Your first call

    Tell us about the kitchen or bathroom, what bothers you about it now, and what you would like to change. Then we set a time to come measure.

  2. 2. Free in-home measure

    We measure the room and look at what the new layout has to work around: plumbing, venting and walls. This is the one meeting at your house.

  3. 3. Layouts and 3D renderings

    Your designer draws layout options for your room and turns them into 3D renderings, so you can see the room before a single material is chosen.

  4. 4. Design meeting at our showroom

    At our Chesterfield showroom you see cabinets, quartz and more than 1,600 tiles in person. Your designer shows three or four options at a time and helps you pick what goes together. Open Saturdays, so you can both come.

  5. 5. Your detailed quote

    About a week after the design meeting, you get a written quote that spells out your layout, the materials you picked and the work to install them.

  6. 6. Sign and build

    One team on your job, design through installation, backed by our 10-year workmanship warranty.
