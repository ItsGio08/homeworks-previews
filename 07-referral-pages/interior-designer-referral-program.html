HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps.
One shared vision.

You stay the creative lead. We handle the measure, ordering, inspection and construction.

Join the program ↗
  1. 01

    Sign up and refer

    Join the program and we give you a dedicated HomeWorks contact. Let them know about a referral before your client’s measure is booked.

  2. 02

    Collaborate on the design

    Your client gets priority scheduling for a free in-home measure, then layout options with 3D renderings. You work alongside our designer to keep the project true to your vision, then we order, inspect and install everything.

  3. 03

    Receive your referral fee

    You receive $500 for every completed project, paid after your client’s final payment.
