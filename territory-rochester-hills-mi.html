PLAN YOUR PROJECT

Rochester Hills
remodeling questions.

What Rochester Hills homeowners ask before a kitchen or bath remodel.

Is the Chesterfield showroom worth the drive from Rochester Hills?

For the design meeting, yes. Your measure happens at your Rochester Hills home, but the design meeting is at our showroom, 49570 Gratiot Ave., where you see your 3D renderings and hold the tile, cabinet doors and quartz you are choosing between. Saturday hours, 9am to 3pm, make it easier for both decision makers to come.

How do you plan a kitchen island that seats four?

We start with the clearance around the island and how it relates to the cabinets and rooms next to it, then size the seating. In Country Club Village, the island seats four and doubles as prep space, with white Shaker cabinets and quartz around it.

Which kitchen storage upgrades are worth it?

The ones you reach for every day. The Bridgewood Farms kitchen used pull-out spice storage, deep cookware drawers, separate trash and recycling pull-outs and full-extension drawers, so everything has a place and the counters stay clear.

Can you make a small primary bathroom in Rochester Hills feel bigger?

Often, yes. In Walnut Creek, a floating vanity showed more floor, a frameless glass shower kept the sightline across the room, and sconces beside the mirror brightened it. Those three choices change how large a compact bathroom feels.
