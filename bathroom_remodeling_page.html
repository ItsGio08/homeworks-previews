Questions we
hear most.

Still wondering about something? Call 888-324-0660.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

It depends on the size of your bathroom, its condition, the layout and your material selections. Moving plumbing or adding features also affects the total. We develop your design and selections first, then prepare a detailed quote.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Most full bathroom remodels take about 4 to 6 weeks once construction begins. Larger bathrooms and detailed tile work can take longer. We go over the schedule before your project starts.

Can you replace my bathtub with a walk-in shower?

Yes. We can turn your tub area into a tiled walk-in shower and add a bench, handheld shower, grab bars or a lower entry to make it easier to use.

Do I have to keep my current layout?

No. We can explore changes that improve storage, comfort and how you use the room, and explain how moving fixtures affects the scope and cost so you can decide.

What warranty comes with my remodel?

HomeWorks provides a 10-year workmanship warranty. Products and fixtures may also carry their own manufacturer warranties.
